LOOT GUARD 1.4.0 — STRICT ELITE POLICY (ENGINE 3.1.0)

Only magic and rare items are evaluated. Any ONE rule below is enough. These
are deliberately conservative stat-combination thresholds, not ordinary upgrades
or a market-price guarantee. Unmatched items retain the game's native filter.
All classes are covered. No LLD exceptions. No botting or automated play.

IAS=attack speed, FCR=cast rate, FRW=movement, FHR=hit recovery, MF=magic find,
ED=enhanced damage (armor ED explicitly means enhanced defense), AR=flat accuracy.
Stats=Strength+Dexterity. Total resists=sum of all four. All-res=lowest of four.
Elemental rolls below mean fire/cold/lightning only; poison cannot substitute.
Skills include All Skills and combine a tree only with its own class.
Implicit base modifiers never count. AND is required unless OR is written.

    "[Testing] Match on common items". It is deliberately broad, and never part
